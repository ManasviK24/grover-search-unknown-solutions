# Research references

These are external publications used as theoretical sources. The papers are **not copied into this repository**. Please consult the original versions via their links.

1. Lov K. Grover, *A Fast Quantum Mechanical Algorithm for Database Search* (1996). [arXiv:quant-ph/9605043](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043).
2. Michel Boyer, Gilles Brassard, Peter Høyer and Alain Tapp, *Tight Bounds on Quantum Searching* (1996). [arXiv:quant-ph/9605034](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605034).
3. Gilles Brassard, Peter Høyer and Alain Tapp, *Quantum Counting* (1998). [arXiv:quant-ph/9805082](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9805082).
4. Gilles Brassard, Peter Høyer, Michele Mosca and Alain Tapp, *Quantum Amplitude Amplification and Estimation* (2000). [arXiv:quant-ph/0005055](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005055).
5. Peter Høyer, *On Arbitrary Phases in Quantum Amplitude Amplification* (2000). [arXiv:quant-ph/0006031](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0006031).
6. Lov K. Grover, Apoorva Patel and Tathagat Tulsi, *Quantum Algorithms with Fixed Points: The Case of Database Search* (2006). [arXiv:quant-ph/0603132](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603132).
7. Theodore J. Yoder, Guang Hao Low and Isaac L. Chuang, *Fixed Point Quantum Search with an Optimal Number of Queries* (2014). [arXiv:1409.3305](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.3305).
8. Pooja Rao and collaborators, *Quantum Amplitude Estimation Algorithms on IBM Quantum Devices* (2020). [arXiv:2008.02102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02102).
9. C. Figgatt and collaborators, *Complete 3 Qubit Grover Search on a Programmable Quantum Computer* (2017). [arXiv:1703.10535](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10535).
